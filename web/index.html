Controls

Click a mapping row, then press the new key. Touch and mouse on the LCD act as the stylus; there is no pointer lock or mouse grab. Brief taps are latched briefly for the emulated ADC; default is 20 ms, with a longer 500 ms latch while a firmware calibration cross is visible so manual calibration clicks are sampled. Touch correction is applied only after calibration, not while the firmware calibration target is visible. Auto calibration assist fast-forwards the initial panel-calibration taps so you do not have to recalibrate your host touchscreen/mouse for every boot.
